      About these numbers

      Reconnections count additional admitted connections for the same sender or receiver, including reopened tabs. They exclude unsuccessful retries. Reservations can include completed tabs and linger for up to 15 minutes after an unreported disconnect.

      The period uses UTC calendar days. Search filters in History affect the transfer list, not this overview.

      Blocked-attempt history begins with this update.

      Monitoring is best effort. Block counters are batched about every 10 seconds; a restart or outage can leave gaps. Activity history can also have missing browser reports.

      Relay bytes count forwarded data once, including key exchange, encrypted signaling, overhead and retries. Direct file bytes are excluded. Observed incoming WebSocket messages include control and rejected messages, but exclude automatic heartbeats. Request units are estimated as messages ÷ 20; HTTP requests, internal calls, alarms, runtime, database usage and other apps are excluded. These are not your account's remaining allowances.

      Relay declines count each transfer at most once per UTC day.

      The daily relay budget closes admission for new relay transfers at the configured threshold. Admitted sessions keep access until their original expiry, including after reconnecting, so traffic can exceed the budget. Updated browsers can still connect directly. Reports flush during activity about every 30 seconds or 512 messages, and on disconnect; quiet sessions may take five minutes. An outage can leave gaps.

      Cleanup runs hourly in bounded batches, with a per-run database write budget. Any backlog, failed run or stale status is shown above; database quotas can delay deletion.
